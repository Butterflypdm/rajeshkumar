A LIFE OF COMMITMENT

Public service is not just a role. It is a responsibility.

N. Rajeshkumar brings together more than two decades of political experience and three decades of involvement in social work and community-oriented initiatives.

As President of Nooranad Gramapanchayat, his public journey is centred on listening to people, understanding local needs and working towards meaningful progress. Alongside public life, he is also known in the vegetable wholesale sector, bringing practical business experience and an understanding of local markets.
